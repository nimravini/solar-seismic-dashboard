Interpretive guardrails

This dashboard can show whether recent events are visually or conditionally consistent with a Marchitelli-style proton-density drop, but it cannot validate the paper. A real replication needs archived proton-density data, a predeclared threshold rule, completeness controls, clustering-aware null models, and a post-2016 holdout test. Interesting goblin, not prophecy.
