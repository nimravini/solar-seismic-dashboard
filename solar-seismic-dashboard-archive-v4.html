Interpretive guardrails

Archive mode is the useful one for historical testing, but it changes the plasma source: OMNI is merged, processed, and time-shifted near-Earth solar wind data, while live mode uses NOAA DSCOVR near-real-time plasma. Treat cross-mode comparisons carefully. A serious replication still needs a predeclared threshold rule, completeness controls, clustering-aware nulls, tectonic regional controls, and a post-selection holdout. Interesting beastie, no prophecy hat.
